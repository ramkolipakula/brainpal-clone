**Ingested:** 2026-11-03

## Overview
BrainPal is a digital wellbeing and screen time intervention companion app for Android. It monitors device usage, detects social media reel consumption, and triggers humorous interventions (memes and roasts) to prevent mindless scrolling and encourage healthy digital habits.

## Scope & Deliverables
- Rebuilt Android native application (`BrainPalRebuilt` / `com.brainrot.rebuilt`)
- Clean-room Jetpack Compose UI matching original BrainPal aesthetics
- Real-time reel detection for Instagram and YouTube Shorts
- Draggable floating capsule counter overlay
- Standalone delivered APK in `delivery/RebuiltApp.apk`
- Complete verification documentation in `reports/`
